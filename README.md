# 👋 WHO AM I?

I'm a **third-semester M.S. student in Computer Science at Korea University**,<br>
working with **Prof. Buru Chang** at [LiLab](https://lilab.korea.ac.kr/home).<br><br>
I'm interested in **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, especially **personalized conversational AI**<br>
that remembers users and adapts to their changing preferences over time.

My research focuses on:

- 🧠 **Long-Term Dialogue Systems** – sustaining memory-aware conversations across sessions
- 🔄 **Preference Tracking** – structuring and updating user preferences from dialogue and behavior
- 🤖 **Agent-Based Long-Term Memory** – deciding what to store, retrieve, update, and forget
- 🖼️ **Multimodal Dialogue Systems** – integrating language, vision, and user context
